# 4C Research Group

**Cognition · Consciousness · Critical Care**

We are a pediatric neurocritical care research group. We study how the injured and critically ill brain works, how we can measure and support consciousness and cognition at the bedside, and how to improve comfort and outcomes for children in intensive care—and for the teams who care for them.

## What we work on

Our research spans four connected areas you can read about in depth on the **Research** section of our site:

- **Neuroprognostication and disorders of consciousness** — Better ways to understand and predict outcomes after acquired brain injury, including work on covert consciousness, neuroimaging, shared data standards, ethics, and family-centred decision-making around devastating brain injuries.

- **ICU delirium and sleep** — How delirium and sleep loss affect the brain in critical illness, including studies of brain connectivity, sleep deprivation in healthcare providers, and tools that might help detect and address these problems earlier.

- **Quantitative EEG and neuromonitoring** — Advanced EEG and nurse-led monitoring to support seizure detection, delirium assessment, and safer, smarter monitoring in the pediatric ICU.

- **Pain, sedation, and comfort** — Multicenter trials and international surveys that aim to improve sedation, analgesia, and comfort for critically ill children, including work on inhaled anesthetics and how neurocritical care is practiced around the world.

We also take part in **large national and international collaborations**—registry work, multicenter trials, and networks that pool effort to answer questions no single centre could answer alone.
